Visiting the website

As things stand, this static website uses no user accounts, no analytics or advertising services and no embedded social feeds. When the site is accessed, the hosting provider may process technically necessary connection data.

Getting in touch

If you use the contact form, your email program opens with a prepared draft. The message is not sent automatically. The details you submit are used to handle your enquiry and are not kept longer than necessary.

Your rights

You may request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data, unless legal obligations prevent this. For questions about data protection, simply send a message to info@bibliotheca-psychonautica.org.
