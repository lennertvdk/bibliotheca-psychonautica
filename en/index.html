What we do

The association and its library

The founding of the Verein Bibliotheca Psychonautica laid the cornerstone for a future foundation whose aim is to bring together and preserve substantial private collections of psychonautic literature, videos, DVDs, CDs, records, pictures, photographs, artworks, correspondence, diaries, research material and manuscripts – in order to build the most comprehensive possible library on drugs and altered states, keeping psychonautic knowledge of altered states of consciousness alive.

Collectors of specialist psychonautic literature can bequeath their private estates to the association or the foundation, in order to preserve this valuable material, make it accessible to other interested people and protect it from self-serving, profit-driven and commercial interests – as well as from careless disposal.

Since July 2013, the association has had shelf space on the premises of Nachtschatten Verlag in Solothurn to build the library. From 2028, a further physical location is to be created in Basel in cooperation with the Gaia Media foundation.

Several extensive collections of books and journals have already arrived and are being catalogued step by step. The list of library holdings recorded so far can be requested from the association. Borrowing books is reserved exclusively for members and is governed by the association's board.
